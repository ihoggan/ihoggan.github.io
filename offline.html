Offline

Use these guides offline

A guide is no use if it disappears when the signal does. Every page here can live on your phone, laptop or a USB stick, and still open with no Wi-Fi, no mobile data and no power to the router.

Checking connection… Checking saved pages…

Three ways to keep it

Phone

Add it to your home screen

Opening any page once saves the whole site on that device. Adding it to your home screen gives it its own icon, so it opens like an app.

  1. Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen or Install app.
  2. iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it once from the new icon while you still have signal. After that it works without.
Laptop or USB

Download the whole site

The complete site as one zip file. Nothing to install and no account needed.

  1. Download the zip (about 10 MB).
  2. Unzip it onto your laptop or a USB stick.
  3. Open index.html in any browser.

A copy on a stick in your grab bag works on any computer, even one that has never been online.

Paper

Print the ones that matter

Paper works when every battery is flat. Open a guide, print it, or choose Save as PDF in the print dialog, and keep it with your kit.

Privacy

What gets stored

Only the public pages of this site, kept by your own browser on your own device. No tracking, no account, nothing sent anywhere. To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
